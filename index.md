Coach's Room Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Coach's Room is an offline football manager career game for Android.

Information we collect

Coach's Room does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDKs. It does not send your career data to a server and does not use cloud storage or network services.

How your data is stored

Your careers, settings, and game progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This local storage is used so the app can restore your careers when you open it again. We do not have access to this data. Uninstalling Coach's Room deletes the app's private storage and the locally stored game data from the device.

Children

Coach's Room is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
